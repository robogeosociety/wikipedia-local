Los Angeles is the most populous city in California and the second-most
populous in the United States, anchoring a metropolitan area of roughly
13 million people across the LA Basin and its valleys. For trip planning it
functions as a constellation of districts — Santa Monica and the coastal
corridor, Downtown, Hollywood, and the theme-park belt stretching south toward
Anaheim — connected by famously car-dependent freeways.
