title: Atlas

---

The atlas is a **gazetteer**: researched, referenced articles about
destinations and points of interest, seeded by trip planning in the travel
vault but owned here. Trip notes stay the planning workspace (dates, bookings,
logistics — never copied to the atlas); each place the trips touch gets a
factual article that outlives the trip.

Articles are written and refreshed by the `wiki_atlas_articles` lane in
obsidian-automations, compiled to `data/atlas.db`, served on the tailnet, and
published to the existing public atlas site.
